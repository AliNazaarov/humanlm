Downloads and likes are HF API values read on 2026-11-01 (HF counts the last 30 days). **[E]** = my estimate. Everything else has a link. This file does not repeat `research/TECH.md`; it goes past it.

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

The exact model in our plan is already built and given away. Since September 2026, at least three teams have released open weights for a 12B or smaller "AI draft → human text" rewriter trained with reverse distillation. The most used, `jialinyyzz/humanizer`, has 264,723 downloads a month and an Apache-2.0 licence. Their recipes agree on four points. Clean pairs matter more than RL. Facts are protected by hard gates. Nobody uses an AI detector in training. RL runs are short and stopped early. So a LoRA on an open model is not a moat any more. It is the starting line. What nobody has is **founder copy** (landing pages, launch emails, LinkedIn/X posts) judged by **skilled human readers** instead of detectors. Build that eval and that data. Do not spend the month training a general humanizer.

## 4. How people benchmark this (and why it is shaky)

1. **AI detectors** (Pangram, Originality, GPTZero) are the default scoreboard for humanizers. Pangram reports 97% accuracy on humanized text in its own posts (https://www.pangram.com/blog/humanizers-announcement). That claim comes from the vendor. The independent NBER check (https://www.nber.org/papers/w34223) found Pangram robust to humanizers. So "beats detectors" will not last, and it is not our pitch anyway.
2. **LLM judges disagree with experts.**
   - Surge's Hemingway-Bench: the EQ-Bench autograder agreed with expert writers "as little as 43%" of the time, across 5,000+ blind pairs judged by professional writers. https://surgehq.ai/blog/hemingway-bench-ai-writing-leaderboard
   - Meta FAIR: plain judges preferred model text over expert text 63.5–84.6% of the time.
   - Lesson: an LLM judge can check **facts and format**. It cannot be trusted on **taste**. That is why jialinyyzz and Smaug use the LLM judge only for facts.
3. **Slop counters** (n-gram ban lists, em dash rate, "not X but Y"). They are useful as a floor. When optimised directly they get gamed: deslop-v4's round 2 scored better and wrote worse.
4. **Copy rate / overlap.** jialinyyzz uses 5-gram + skeleton overlap. GoHumanize uses "% of words kept". This catches the most common failure: handing the draft back almost unchanged.
5. **Small expert human panels** are the only thing anyone trusts for quality. FAIR used 18–20 pairs. Chakrabarty & Dhillon (CHI 2026) used 28 MFA writers and 131 lay readers. Fine-tuning on an author's complete works flipped experts from preferring human text 82.7% of the time to **preferring the AI 62%**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18353 (2026-01-26). That is strong evidence that the "personal voice" v2 works if there is enough of the person's text.

## 6. Recipe to steal (step by step, for founder copy)

1. **Week 1, day 1–2: baseline the open models before training anything.**
   - Run `jialinyyzz/humanizer` (Apache-2.0), `abacusai/Smaug-Humanizer` (Apache-2.0), the `blader/humanizer` skill on a frontier model, and Hemmingway's free app (look only, no login if one is needed) on our 200-prompt founder-copy eval.
   - If one of them already wins our blind test, the job is fine-tuning on top of it. Cost about $0–20 on a rented GPU **[E]**.
2. **Pick the base.** First choice: `jialinyyzz/humanizer` weights. It is a Gemma-4-12B base already trained on 28.6k pairs plus DPO and GRPO, and it is Apache-2.0, so you can continue training from it. Check: part of its data is CC-BY-NC (PERSUADE, wikiHow). The weights are Apache-2.0, but hl-critic should rule on it. Fallback: Gemma-4-12B base, which both 12B teams chose over the instruct version.
3. **Build pairs the Smaug/jialinyyzz way, on founder copy.**
   - Human side: launch emails, landing pages, founder posts dated ≤ 2022-11-30, licence-checked.
   - AI side: 3+ different frontier models, each told to "rewrite as a typical AI chatbot would, keep every fact, rebuild every sentence".
4. **Filter hard. This is the biggest lever.**
   - Drop near-copy pairs (sentence overlap).
   - Require the same layout.
   - LLM fact check (facts only).
   - Dedupe.
   - Drop post-cutoff dates.
   - 3–5k clean pairs beat 30k dirty ones (Smaug used 3,676).
5. **SFT.** LoRA r=64 α=128, 1–2 epochs, loss on the target tokens only. For scale: GoHumanize did 2.6k pairs on a 4B model in 8 minutes on one H100. A 12B LoRA on 5k pairs should be about 1–3 H100-hours **[E]**.
6. **DPO, small.** Use pairs from your own checkpoint's outputs. Choose on facts + copy rate (jialinyyzz), plus your daily picks for taste. Do not use detector scores.
7. **Optional GRPO, short.**
   - Reward = Smaug kernel style reward (close to the human original, far from the AI draft) − jialinyyzz copy penalty.
   - Hard gates set the reward to the group minimum: numbers, names, layout, length 0.7–1.4×, spelling.
   - **Stop at 100–200 steps.** Smaug saw quality fall at 400.
8. **Handle structural tells at inference, not in the weights.** FTPO failed on em dashes, bullets, headers and emoji. Use proportional (human-rate) bans in a sampler or a post-check. Use FTPO only for word-level tells.
9. **Judge with people.**
   - Automatic gates: facts, grammar, copy rate, slop counters inside the human band.
   - Then a blind panel of skilled readers: you plus 3–5 copywriters, 50+ pairs per checkpoint.
   - Report detector rates only as a side number.
10. **Fix the genre gap everyone admits.** Social posts with emoji, hashtags and threads are the weakest genre for jialinyyzz (3/16 flagged). LinkedIn/X posts are exactly our wedge. Gather them deliberately.
11. **Personal voice (v2).** Chakrabarty & Dhillon show fine-tuning on one author's full body of work beats experts. Founders have far less text, so start with in-context samples + style profile, then a per-user LoRA once a user has about 50+ pieces **[E]**.

## What to steal
- The jialinyyzz data card format: every source, licence tier, date cutoff and drop count is published. Copy it for `data/`.
- Smaug's filters, 5 gates and "fail a gate = group minimum" trick. Its embedding style reward, which needs no judge for taste.
- jialinyyzz's copy penalty (5-gram + syntactic skeleton, free below 0.22) as an eval metric from day one.
- Thoughtworks' proportional bans (p = 1 − human/model) so tells land at the human rate, not zero.
- FAIR's lesson: LLM judges for facts, humans for taste, and always cap length.
- AGENTS.md + a byte-exact prompt + a self-test hash. This is how jialinyyzz makes its model easy for agents to adopt, which matters for our API.
- The public audit-record style of HumanizerBench, for our own public blind leaderboard.

## What it changes for us
- **"We have our own model" is no longer a pitch.** Free Apache-2.0 12B rewriters with 260k+ monthly downloads do the general job. Our edge must be **founder copy quality as judged by skilled readers**, plus the pick-data loop and voice. A general humanizer won't do it.
- **PLAN_30D week 2 changes.** Don't train a v0.1 from scratch. Baseline jialinyyzz/Smaug/blader first, then fine-tune the best Apache-2.0 one on founder copy. That saves about a week and most of the GPU budget **[E]**.
- **The eval is the product moat.** The public benchmarks measure detector bypass. Nobody publishes a human-judged founder-copy benchmark. Ship one in month 1 (it is already in PLAN week 4).
- **Hemmingway is the closest positioned rival** (app + API, everyday messages). It sells "undetectable". We sell "copy people trust and click". Watch its pricing and licence (CC-BY-NC).
- **Budget fits easily.** Every recipe here ran on one GPU or a laptop. Expect well under $500 for month 1 GPU **[E]**. The scarce resource is clean founder copy and skilled-reader time, not compute.
- **Open question for hl-critic:** can we continue training on jialinyyzz/humanizer weights for a commercial product, given that part of its training data is non-commercial?
