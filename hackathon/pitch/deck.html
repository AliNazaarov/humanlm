Approach · hackathon/harness/harness.py + gates.py

Draft, verify, fix only what failed. The verifier is also the reward.

1 · DraftGLM-5.3 Flash on Fireworks serverless, one shared system prompt, temp 0.7.
2 · VerifyFour gates from pipeline/metrics.py: nothing invented, nothing lost, no AI tells (human band), length 0.7-1.3x.
3 · Targeted fix"Fix only these problems: you dropped 2,047; remove 'seamlessly'." Up to 3 revisions.
4 · Keep bestFewest failed gates wins; ties go to the later revision. Report shipped with the text.
↩ loop back to 1 while any gate fails

Training

  • 5,000 licence-clean pairs: Common Pile human text; an open model wrote the AI draft; we train draft → human (reverse distillation).
  • SFT on Fireworks: see TRAIN_LOG.md for the job state.
  • RL: the same four gates as reward, so the model learns to pass first time and the loop becomes a safety net.

What makes it honest

  • Same prompt, same 250 items, every run file scored, nothing dropped.
  • A fairness row: Claude Opus 5.5 + the same harness.
  • A no-rewrite control: copying the draft scores 32%.
  • An independent audit with signals the loop never sees (next slide but one).

Business · who pays and why it compounds

Copy that sounds like you, with a guarantee the claims did not move.

Who pays

  • Founders and marketers publishing landing pages, launch and cold emails, LinkedIn and X posts.
  • Agencies that turn out hundreds of drafts a week and sign off on every claim.
  • Agent builders who need a publish-safe step before an agent posts on a company's behalf.

The pain

AI copy reads fake and loses trust. The tools that fix the style change the facts, and a changed claim in marketing copy is an FTC exposure, not a typo.

Pricing logic

Per rewrite, with the verifier report attached: what was kept, what was checked. Open-model cost of about per 1,000 rewrites against for the frontier leaves room for a margin and a free tier.

Moat

  • Data: licence-clean reverse-distillation pairs from Common Pile; no frontier text, ever.
  • Verifier: the same code is eval, product guarantee and RL reward.
  • Loop: every pick or edit a user makes is preference data for the next model.
Not a humanizer: no detector is ever used as a reward or sold as evasion6 / 7
