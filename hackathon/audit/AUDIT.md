## C. Gaming checks (all systems; the harness rows are the ones under suspicion)

## H. Caveats the team should say out loud

- G1/G2 are regex proxies, not fact checkers. Section E shows how many fails are artefacts; quote the lenient rate when a judge pushes.
- The human band's `para_opener_repeat` and `one_line_para_share` ranges are [0, 0], so marketing formats with `Headline:`/`CTA:` lines fail them by construction; that is why even the held-out human originals do not score 100% on the hold-out tells.
- chrF to the human original rewards staying close to the source wording; a pure copy of the draft scores the floor, a good rewrite can legitimately move away. Read A together with C1.
- The dev/trick drafts were written by Claude Opus 5.5; the Claude rows are rewriting their own family's text. The held-out split (real human originals, drafts by an open model) does not have that bias.
- Nothing here is a human preference judgement. The pairwise judge and the blind human test in `hackathon/report/` are the only humanness evidence.
